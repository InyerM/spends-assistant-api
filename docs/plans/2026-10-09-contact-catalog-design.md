# Contacts and merchants

The owner approved both transfer recipients and purchase merchants, plus a scan of every confirmed transaction. The directory groups exact, owner-scoped identities; suffix-only bank identifiers cannot establish identity. Transactions remain the source of movement counts and currency-separated totals. Existing financial rows and balances must remain unchanged.

Use Anotto's existing dark surfaces, shared search and form controls, and semantic status colors. The UI/UX search returned marketing patterns rather than a suitable directory pattern; existing product conventions are the verified fallback. Label the section Contacts and merchants. Cards show an editable name, identity type, masked identifier, movement count and last activity. A detail view lists linked transactions and category distribution. Suggestions say Based on your history, with confirmation required before financial posting.

Persist contacts and transaction links with composite owner foreign keys and RLS. An owner-scoped SQL scan processes pages of active transactions, records found, ambiguous or missing evidence for each row, and is restartable. Extract explicit full destination accounts, Nequi and payment keys from source evidence; parse merchants from completed purchase evidence. Preserve identifier namespaces, retain leading zeros, and never merge short suffixes or ambiguous names automatically. Automatic extraction runs on future confirmed transaction changes. Manual names survive rescanning. Deleted transactions do not contribute to totals. Multi-currency amounts are never added together.

Release and validate the web contract before mobile adoption. The migration and private production audit compare financial snapshots before and after; all source exports and actual identifiers remain outside Git. Tests cover exact identities, incoming source exclusion, ambiguous destinations, leading zeros, idempotent scans, ownership, rename and currency-separated totals.
