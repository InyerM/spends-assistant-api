# Evidence to reconstruct Tyba Pocket and Binance P2P activity

This guide describes source records to collect before entering reviewed investment events. It contains no personal amounts or account identifiers. The web investment journal currently accepts manually confirmed entries; the image document inbox is separate and does not populate that journal automatically.

## Tyba Pocket

1. Open Tyba Pocket in the app or [Tyba's transaction portal](https://tyba.com.co/inversiones/). Find the Pocket movement history. Capture each contribution and withdrawal detail with its date, amount, product name, and reference. Tyba states that the app and portal show investment movements, but its public pages do not specify a universal receipt-download menu. If the current interface offers a statement or transaction receipt, save it; otherwise retain screenshots of the movement details and ask Tyba support for the relevant account movement statement.
2. Collect the matching bank debit for each contribution and the matching bank credit for each withdrawal. Record the exact amounts, dates, and source/destination account labels. A withdrawal used to repay a loan still needs its own loan payment evidence and principal/interest/fee allocation.
3. Capture a dated position value before withdrawal and after it if available. Do not infer realized return from rounded amounts or assume a remaining balance is zero without evidence. Tyba describes Pocket as an investment product; its site identifies the underlying fund on its [investment page](https://tyba.com.co/inversiones/).

## Binance P2P

1. On the [Binance website](https://www.binance.com/en/support/faq/detail/8d82e480bff74a75a7709e3c70e17379), open **P2P → All Orders → Completed**. Open the buy and sell orders and download each **Receipt** when the button is available. Binance says direct receipt download is website-only; its app can request receipts through **Support → Self Service → Buy/Sell Crypto (Fiat/P2P) → P2P Order Receipts**.
2. Capture each order's ID, completion time, actual asset and quantity, total COP paid or received, executed rate, status, and any fee. Keep the corresponding bank debit/credit evidence. If an order was split, capture every completed order rather than only the approximate total.
3. Keep a dated Binance asset balance around the purchase and sale to identify any remainder. A COP withdrawal is not itself proof that the full token position was sold. Do not substitute the market TRM for the actual P2P execution rate.

## Intake and review boundary

The local web document flow accepts private PNG, JPEG, and WebP images up to 5 MiB, classifies their content, and creates an OCR review draft. PDF statement upload and automatic linkage from a document to an investment or loan event remain unreleased. Until that link exists, keep the source file reference with the manually reviewed journal entry and do not create duplicate cash expenses for contributions, redemptions, or P2P settlement legs.
